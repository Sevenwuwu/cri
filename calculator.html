Taiwan Renewable Energy Act - Rooftop Solar mandates

根據最新三讀修正通過條文：新建、增建或改建之建築物，達一定規模（例如屋頂面積 300 坪以上者），起造人應設置一定比率之太陽光電發電設備。

According to recent amendments: New, extended, or reconstructed buildings exceeding specified sizes (e.g. roof spaces above 300 pings) must install rooftop solar arrays under legal mandates.

CRi 柔性面板優勢： 許多老舊或新設計建築因耐震法規限制「無法承受傳統重型鋼架與玻璃面板」（每坪重量達60-80kg）。CRi 柔性板（每坪僅約9.2kg，為傳統的1/8）能完美通過建管處耐震承載審查，不需重新補強梁柱，並可浮貼免鑽孔防漏水，是法規合規與結構安全的唯一首選。

CRi's Light Advantage: Standard panels load 60-80kg per ping, failing earthquake loading code reviews on many roofs. CRi modules weigh just 9.2kg per ping (1/8 of traditional solar), passing loading permits easily without pillar reinforcements.
