Secure Non-China Supply Chain

為防範國際貿易壁壘與資安、ESG 合規風險，CRi 輕質柔性太陽能組件建立自主可控的供應鏈，原料主要來自台灣及美歐領先材料大廠，確保優異耐久性。

To mitigate trade barriers and comply with strict ESG regulations, CRi established a reliable supply chain partnering with leading US, European, and Taiwanese manufacturers.

  • 封裝膠膜 (POE/EVA)：Encapsulation Film: SABIC 生產之高品質高透水阻隔 POE 封裝層。 High density water-barrier POE encapsulation layers by SABIC.

  • 防水接線盒 (Junction Box)：Junction Box: 美商 TE Connectivity / Enphase Energy 高規格 IP68 智慧接線盒。 IP68 high-spec junction boxes by US TE Connectivity / Enphase Energy.

Power UK策略結盟

英國與歐洲能源基礎建設與 AFC 項目合作夥伴

UK & Europe Grid Infrastructure & AFC Asset Partner

CRi Energy 與英國能源基礎建設開發商 Power UK (poweruk.uk) 達成深度設備供應與聯合開發協議。 本協議重點在於配合 Power UK 於英國東部（East of England）推進的 250 MW+ 大型電網側儲能（BESS）項目管線。 我們為該管線項目提供符合英國 G99 配電併網規範之 3.44MWh 貨櫃儲能系統，並高度整合華城電機（Fortune Electric）擁有 KEMA 短路試驗合格的電力變壓器與台達電 PCS，為歐洲電網調頻與備用電力市場提供堅實的硬體基石。

CRi Energy has partnered with UK-based energy developer Power UK (poweruk.uk). This collaboration supports Power UK's 250 MW+ utility-scale BESS pipeline in the East of England. CRi supplies G99-compliant 3.44MWh containerized systems integrated with Fortune Electric's KEMA-certified grid-transformers and Delta PCS controllers, backed by Munich Re bankability reviews for the European capacity markets.
