Advanced monocrystalline panels designed for corrugated roofs, curved structures, and load-limited buildings.

為什麼選擇 輕質柔性太陽能？

Why Choose Lightweight Flexible Solar?

針對高齡廠房、承重受限、曲面結構與嚴格的法規要求，提供免鑽孔、零漏水風險的唯一解方。

The ultimate solar solution for load-limited roofs, curved structures, and strict net-zero mandates.

破解屋頂承重限制

Overcoming Load Barriers

許多老舊鋼構廠房、大型倉庫屋頂的額外載重限額僅為 5-10 kg/m²。傳統玻璃模組（加鋼架達 15-20 kg/m²）無法通過結構安全技師認證，而 CRi 柔性組件（低於 2.7 kg/m²）可輕鬆過關。

Many older warehouse and factory roofs can only support an additional 5-10 kg/m². Standard glass panels fail weight audits; CRi modules (under 2.7 kg/m²) pass safety codes easily.

免鑽孔 · 零漏水隱憂

Zero Drilling, No Leaks

傳統安裝需在鐵皮浪板上鑽孔固定鋼骨支架，極易導致日後鏽蝕與滲水。CRi 採用氣候級高粘度結構耐候背膠直接黏貼浮貼，完全無損浪板結構與原廠防水防漏保固。

Traditional brackets require roof drilling, causing water leak risks. CRi panels are bonded flat using high-viscosity structural adhesives with zero penetrations.

用電大戶與法規強制趨勢

Global Net-Zero Policies

台灣用電大戶條款強制契約容量大於 5000kW 企業設置 10% 綠能；歐盟 Solar Rooftop Mandate 規定 2026 起新商用建築強制安裝太陽能。輕質柔性組件是舊建物實現法規合規與 ESG 指標的最快路徑。

Taiwan's Green Energy Act mandates heavy energy consumers to install solar; EU's Rooftop Mandate enforces solar on public buildings from 2026. Flexible solar provides the fastest path to ESG compliance.
