Industrial Roof

1. 隆興冷凍廠屋頂光儲改裝

1. Longxing Cold Storage Roof

解決傳統剛性光電鑽孔漏水痛點，以 560W 柔性板直接黏貼浪板波峰。免鑽孔、零漏水風險，耐 17 級強颱。配合 344kWh 儲能櫃進行每日峰谷差避峰套利，大幅節省年電費。

Avoided roof leaks from mechanical drilling by bonding 560W flexible panels directly onto metal ridges. Paired with 344kWh liquid-cooled storage for daily peak-shaving, saving millions annually.

Agriculture

2. 設施農業綠色溫室大棚

2. Curved Glass Agricultural Greenhouse

溫室結構輕巧、不耐重型玻璃板與抗風支架。採用高透光、防眩光的 240W 柔性板貼合圓弧玻璃頂部，實現綠色發電與部分遮陽，同時維持棚內溫濕度微控系統供電。

Laminated 240W flexible panels onto curved greenhouse glasses. Avoided structural reinforcement costs while supplying solar backup power for automated temperature and water networks inside.

BIPV Wall

3. 都市帷幕大樓立面整合 (BIPV)

3. Commercial High-Rise BIPV Wall

利用專利雙面吸光型光伏電池結構，貼合於高層寫字樓南向立面帷幕玻璃。抗強風壓、防眩光，可吸收地面漫射光，比普通單面柔性板提升 12% 發電增益，降低大樓夏日空調負荷。

Applied double-sided absorption panels directly onto vertical high-rise glass cladding. Resists high wind loads while leveraging ground reflections to deliver 12% more power yield than single-sided panels.

Carport & ESS

4. 智慧光儲充一體化車棚

4. EV Smart Charging Solar Carport

柔性單晶光電直接黏貼於輕鋼構車棚弧形頂板上，配置 344kWh 智能液冷儲能櫃與快充樁。在白天光照期儲電，傍晚快充高峰放電，解決快充站變壓器容量限額痛點，平滑電網衝擊。

Fitted flexible solar modules directly onto curved steel carport decks. Integrated with 344kWh liquid-cooled batteries to discharge during peak fast-charging rushes, easing pressure on distribution grids.

Station BESS

5. 中油桃園啟東加油站 [50kWh]

5. CPC Gas Station BESS, Taoyuan

中油指標性安全示範案。採用 e-LiFe (易來福) 具防爆安全認證之軟碳 (soft carbon) 技術 LFP 電芯，完美通過加油站極嚴格之消防與防爆防雷安規檢測，穩定供應加油機與站區後備電源。

Taiwan CPC's milestone safety project. Deployed e-LiFe LFP packs with soft carbon tech, fully certified under gas station fire, lightning and explosion hazards, securing stable backup grid power.

Utility ESS

6. 苗栗/彰化/高雄工商業貨櫃儲能案場

6. C&I Container Storage, Taiwan

包含苗栗 1.1MWh、彰化 2MWh、高雄 1MWh 工商企業端用電大戶專案。引進士林電機與台達電 PCS 電網控制櫃，配置微通道精密液冷控制，溫差小於 3℃，協助廠商落實淨零碳排指標。

Completed Miaoli (1.1MWh), Changhua (2MWh), and Kaohsiung (1MWh) for heavy electricity consumers. Installed Shihlin Electric & Delta PCS, managing temp deviations under 3℃ to secure longer cycle life.

Global BIPV

7. 杜拜 Mall of Emirates 2MW 車棚

7. Dubai Mall of Emirates 2MW Carport

阿聯酋指標大型光電項目。利用高分子前蓋板與抗風沙 POE 封裝結構，克服中東地區極端高溫與沙塵侵蝕，大幅減少了車棚框架結構的負重，至今已平穩運行多年。

Iconic UAE commercial solar array. Outfitted with high-transmittance membranes and anti-sandstorm POE layers to withstand desert heats, saving structural reinforcement costs across carports.

Overlay Repower

8. 舊光電電站就地改裝 (2.0倍增益)

8. Station repowering overlay retrofits

老舊屋頂電站玻璃面板效率退化至原先 10%。在不拆除原鋼架的基礎下，直接以氣候結構膠將 560W 柔性單晶板覆貼於失效面板上。原地發電容量翻倍，發電效益顯著增長。

Retrofitting degraded crystalline panels. Affixed lightweight CRi 560W modules flat over existing useless glass structures. Doubled the solar capacity without bracket reconstruction expenses.
