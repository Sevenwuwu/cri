Changhua, Taiwan. 12MW / 45.31MWh solar PV + BESS grid smoothing. Combines lightweight solar panel output with containerized BESS arrays.

US Grid

7. 美國工商業光儲一體化項目

7. US Commercial Solar-Storage

配置於美國多個工業園區的 BTM 配儲項目。具備智慧削峰填谷與電力備份功能，符合 IEEE 1547 標準。

USA. 2.3MW / 10MWh PV + BESS integration, delivering dynamic charge management and power backings.

Agri-PV

8. Agricultural Circular Solar-BESS

圓弧玻璃溫室專利貼合項目。利用 240W 輕質柔性板直接黏貼，兼顧高透光發電與作物避光遮陽，搭配離網儲能櫃供電。

Curved glass frames fitted with 240W flexible modules. Pairs with battery storage to supply greenhouse micro-systems.

UK BESS

9. 英國東部 250MW 電網側大型儲能項目

9. UK 250MW Grid-Side BESS (East of England)

Jointly developed with UK infrastructure partner Power UK. Integrates e-LiFe BESS with Fortune Electric KEMA-certified transformers, fully compliant with UK G99 grid connection codes, Munich Re technical audits, and Approved Document B fire standards.
