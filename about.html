Patent No. I746034

專利名稱： 太陽能電池單元，其製備方法及含被之太陽能電池模組。
本專利界定了高分子膜材包覆單晶電池片、無損貼合與無應力封裝工法，確保組件在大曲率彎曲（半徑0.5m）時電池內部不產生隱裂。

Patent Name: Solar cell unit, fabrication method, and module encapsulation. Defines stress-free polymer encapsulation layout, ensuring zero cell micro-cracks under large bending curvature (radius 0.5m).

Patent No. I850761

專利名稱： 雙面吸光型光伏電池結構。
為垂直壁面貼合 BIPV 與大棚反射光設計，前後蓋板皆採用高透光高分子材料，充分收集反射散射光，比一般單面柔性板提升 8-15% 的日照發電增益。

Patent Name: Double-sided absorption photovoltaic cell structures. Designed for vertical BIPV wall panels and agricultural greenhouses, collecting ambient reflection to add 8-15% generation gains.

非中資供應鏈安全結構

Secure Non-China Supply Chain

為防範國際貿易壁壘與資安、ESG 合規風險，CRi 輕質柔性太陽能組件建立自主可控的供應鏈，原料主要來自台灣及美歐領先材料大廠，確保優異耐久性。

To mitigate trade barriers and comply with strict ESG regulations, CRi established a reliable supply chain partnering with leading US, European, and Taiwanese manufacturers.

  • 高分子前蓋板：Front Membrane: SABIC / 3M 提供之高透光抗紫外塑料前板。 SABIC / 3M high-transmittance anti-UV protective polymer sheets.
  • 封裝膠膜 (POE/EVA)：Encapsulation Film: SABIC 生產之高品質高透水阻隔 POE 封裝層。 High density water-barrier POE encapsulation layers by SABIC.
  • 銀漿焊帶線：Ribbon Wire: 台灣昇貿科技 (Shenmao Technology Inc.) 低溫無鉛環保焊帶。 Low-temperature lead-free ribbons by Taiwan Shenmao Technology.
  • 複合碳纖維背板：Carbon Backsheet: 台灣台塑集團福懋興業 (Formosa Taffeta) 製造之高剛性耐候碳纖維板。 Formosa Taffeta (Taiwan Formosa Plastics Group) durable carbon fiber panels.
  • 防水接線盒 (Junction Box)：Junction Box: 美商 TE Connectivity / Enphase Energy 高規格 IP68 智慧接線盒。 IP68 high-spec junction boxes by US TE Connectivity / Enphase Energy.
